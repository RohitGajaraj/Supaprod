# Rename brief: life after Cadence

> _Created: 2026-07-04 · Last updated: 2026-07-04_

Cadence is dead as a name. An $80B EDA company with 149 registered marks and a documented demand-letter habit, plus Uber's open-source orchestration engine squatting the exact developer mindshare we live in, plus "the cadence" being a generic SaaS operating term. Any LLM asked about "Cadence" answers about someone else. That last one is the killer for an AI-native product in 2026. Here is where we land instead.

## 1. The shortlist, ranked

### 1. Selvedge
**Pitch.** The self-finished edge of woven fabric, the part that cannot unravel.
**Soul fit.** This is the moat drawn as a word: the product's value is the finished, permanent edge of the weave, the record of decisions and outcomes that holds everything else together. It inherits the Loom design language natively, and it carries the premium-denim connotation of "the well-made record." Nobody else in software means anything by it.
**Reality check.** Cleanest search signal of all 16 candidates checked: one tiny consultancy (spelled Selvage), one London VC, one textiles magazine at selvedge.org. No SaaS product found. Real risks: the selvedge/selvage spelling fork, and no registrar or trademark database has actually been queried yet.
**Logo.** The Loom Knot. A trefoil of one continuous thread with true over-under weaving; a knot that cannot be untied without cutting. Name and mark tell the same sentence.

### 2. Throughline
**Pitch.** The through-line from decision to outcome. It is literally the pitch.
**Soul fit.** Says the decision-OS story in plain English; three syllables, spells itself, and enterprise buyers get it in one pass. Slightly more descriptive, slightly less ownable-feeling than Selvedge.
**Reality check.** throughline.com is a DC services consultancy, not software; NPR's Throughline podcast is a discoverability tax, not a legal threat. No software product found. .com is taken; throughline.ai showed nothing in search signal.
**Logo.** Mark 4 (the Throughline sketch, built for it): thin strands converge on a point, one bold line leaves. Thin means considered, bold means chosen, and that grammar extends into the whole product's iconography.

### 3. Weft
**Pitch.** The thread the shuttle carries across the warp; one pass = one product loop.
**Soul fit.** The purest loom word, four letters, and every cycle of sense-decide-ship-learn is one pass of the weft. Gorgeous fit.
**Reality check.** Crowded at the small end: weft.ai (commerce), weft-cli (AI dev agents), letsweft.com (task manager, adjacent). Nobody big, but the domains are picked over and three small AI tools already answer to the name. If chosen, move fast and out-market them.
**Logo.** Loom Knot, or the Decision Path glyphs as the in-product system.

### 4. Rondo
**Pitch.** The musical form that keeps returning to its theme, the loop without Cadence's baggage.
**Soul fit.** Keeps the musical heritage of the current name, warm, two syllables, spells itself. Weaker on the memory/receipts story than the loom words.
**Reality check.** No PM or dev-tool collisions; Rondo Energy ($162M) and a purchasing SaaS at getrondo.ai exist but nowhere near us. A genuine sleeper if the weave identity ever feels too precious.
**Logo.** The Decision Path: open ring, heavy node, closing ring; the loop that returns and gets checked.

### 5. Cairn
**Pitch.** Stones stacked by those who came before to mark the route.
**Soul fit.** Decisions as trail markers left for the next traveler is a beautiful memory story. But getcairn.dev is dev-adjacent, two open-source agents share the name, and "cairn vs carn" costs us spelling.
**Logo.** Decision Path (stacked markers read naturally).

Dropped with cause: Loom (Atlassian, $975M), Heddle (heddle.us is a near-direct competitor), Verdict/Upshot/Crux (occupied by funded AI startups or heavyweight namesakes).

## 2. My recommendation

**Selvedge, with the Loom Knot mark.** It is the only candidate where the name, the moat, and the design system we already built are the same idea: a weave whose finished edge cannot unravel, drawn as a knot that cannot be untied. It had the cleanest collision field of everything searched, it sounds premium to an enterprise buyer, and no LLM will ever confuse us with anyone.

## 3. Runner-up

**Throughline, with mark 4.** If Selvedge fails at the trademark or registrar stage, Throughline is the safest strong name: the pitch is in the word, the collision field is services-and-podcast only, and the convergence mark was practically designed for it.

## 4. Verify before committing

1. **Trademark.** USPTO + EUIPO knockout search, classes 9 and 42, for both SELVEDGE and SELVAGE (the spelling fork must be searched and ideally defensively registered). Everything above is search-signal heuristics; no database was queried.
2. **Domains and handles.** Registrar check on selvedge.com/.ai/.io, getselvedge.com, plus GitHub org, npm scope, and X/LinkedIn handles. Same sweep for Throughline as the fallback.
3. **Coexistence check.** Confirm Selvedge Magazine (textiles) and Selvedge Venture (VC) are non-conflicting classes; get a formal opinion letter before spending on brand.
4. **Rename cost, honestly.** We have done two renames already and both left legacy identifiers unmigrated by design. Budget the same here: user-facing name, routes, landing, docs, and the DB/env/slug layer get a documented equivalence ruling, not a big-bang migration. One disclaimer block in CLAUDE.md, redirects for public routes, and the sanitizer list updated. Two focused days, not two weeks.

The butterfly retires with honor; the Facet Butterfly stays in the drawer as the continuity option if we ever get cold feet on changing name and mark at once. We should not.
