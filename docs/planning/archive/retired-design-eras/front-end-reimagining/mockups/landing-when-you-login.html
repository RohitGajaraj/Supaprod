Frame A · where each question is answered
1
What waits on me?
The needs-you pill on Approvals and the ember node on the Spine are the same gate object: one count, one source. Clicking either opens the same gate card in the Thread, and approving from any of the three clears all of them.
2
What happened while I was away?
The Briefing at the top of today in the Thread: receipts first, then the one thing that waits. Everything since you left sits below it as receipts, in order, with believable timestamps.
3
Where do I jump?
Every Spine node is a door: done nodes open their artifact on the Canvas, the ember node opens your call. The journey chips in the Composer start a new slice, and hovering one pre-lights the Spine stages it would run.
4
How do I ask anything?
The Ask button in the TopBar (always visible, with its key) and the docked Composer are one input: Ask focuses the Composer, it never opens a second box. Ask, act, navigate, or start a journey from the same line.
