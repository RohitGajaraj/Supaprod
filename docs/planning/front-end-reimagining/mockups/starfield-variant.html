Recommendation · A1, now with rendered evidence

Adopt the scoped app-idle variant as shown in Frame 2. At 18 far-layer stars capped at 0.24 opacity it genuinely is subtle: from normal viewing distance Frame 2 reads as Frame 1 with a faint sense that the room is the same canvas the landing was drawn on, and the brand continuity is worth that whisper on a surface that is otherwise a dead end of black. The honest caveats: on a bright, high-contrast monitor the three or four largest stars are individually noticeable near the headline, and if that registers as decoration rather than atmosphere the correct fix is to cap star radius at 0.10 in the app preset, not to dim further, because below roughly 0.08 opacity the field stops existing and the variant becomes a placebo. If you find yourself squinting to check whether the stars are there, that is the argument for ticking the first box instead; a field nobody can see is complexity without a payoff. Either answer is safe: the boundary that actually matters, working surfaces never carry stars, holds in both worlds and Frame 3 shows it.
