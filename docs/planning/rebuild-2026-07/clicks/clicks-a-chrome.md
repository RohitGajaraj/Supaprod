## The two shells, in one picture (context for everything below)

`src/routes/_authenticated.tsx:146-185` picks the shell from a hardcoded pathname allowlist:

```ts
const isReimaginedSurface =
  isMissionControl ||
  pathname === "/threads" || pathname.startsWith("/threads/") ||
  pathname === "/artifacts" || pathname.startsWith("/artifacts/") ||
  pathname === "/settings" || pathname.startsWith("/settings/") ||
  pathname === "/approvals" || pathname.startsWith("/approvals/") ||
  pathname === "/brain" || pathname.startsWith("/brain/");
```

- **Shell 1, "Obsidian" (`AppShell.tsx`, 1033 lines):** 248px left rail with 12 destinations,
  Search button, per-page opt-in `TopBar`, mobile bottom nav, `FocusDock`, `GotoShortcuts` (keys
  0-9 / s / a), `FlowWidget`, `AuditLineageSheet`, `MachineViewContainer`. Serves ~68 route files
  (34 of them are `throw redirect` stubs).
- **Shell 2, "room" (`RoomChromeShell` + `MissionShellView`):** 52px ink top bar with 4 doors,
  `AccountMenu`, `ProductSwitcher`, plus (in the room only) the Spine, Thread, Canvas,
  `WorkingStrip`, docked Composer, `ApprovalsTray`, `CrewDrawer`, `RoomTour`. Serves 7 paths.

`GotoShortcuts`, `FocusDock` and `AppShell` are all switched off on shell 2. Nothing carries the
loop's 10 other destinations across. That single fact generates roughly a third of this list.

## The three fixes that buy the most demo safety

1. **A1 + A19** - stop rendering `1/2/3/⏎` hints on gate cards outside the tray. Today, pressing
   `1` on a gate card in the room jumps the canvas to Discover. This is the one that will happen on
   camera.
2. **A3 + A12** - give the room shell a door back to the loop. Any click on Settings, Brain or
   Approvals currently strands the user in a shell with four destinations and no way back to the
   product's own seven stages except the browser back button.
3. **A2 + A15** - make "Search" and "Ask" tell the truth. The rail button labelled Search opens an
   Ask dialog; the room's Ask dialog throws away the box you typed into and streams the answer into
   a 380px column below three other cards. That is precisely the founder's call-out.
