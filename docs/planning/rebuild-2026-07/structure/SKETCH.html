Density

Compact with breathing space is a contrast rather than a padding value. Tight inside a group, generous between groups. The same spacing value everywhere is what makes an interface read as either cramped or empty with nothing in between.

Hierarchy comes from weight and luminance before size, and from size before any box or colour. Radii are concentric and computed, inner equals outer minus the gap, and they cap at 12 to 16px so cards do not round into soft blobs. Every number that can change uses tabular figures so rows never jitter. Floor of 12px on anything a person reads, which this document now obeys.

A distinct typographic identity at the threshold, and then the product becomes an instrument inside. Sign in, sign up, reset, invite accept. Nowhere else.
Asked directly: should Pixel carry every number, duration, count, diff and identifier? No. The five variants on disk are decorative pixel-grid styles. At 12 to 13px in a dense table the strokes drop out, tabular figures are unreliable, and reading quantity in it is tiring. It reads as retro game rather than as an instrument, and this product is mostly numbers. Scoping it to the door is what saves it.

Three weights maximum across the system. Two families, since one family for everything flattens the hierarchy and more than two is noise. Tracking tightened optically on display sizes, opened on short uppercase labels, and left alone everywhere else. Every remaining font-pixel use outside the auth surfaces gets removed.

The voice law

Never greet, always report. The first line is a fact rather than a pleasantry. If a surface has something true and specific to say, it says that instead of a category label. Nothing is ever addressed to "the user".

Generic greeting language exists because generic products have nothing to say at that moment. This product knows what happened, so it can say it.

Three rules that keep the voice from becoming its own kind of slop. A fact rather than a joke, since a line doing personality instead of information has failed. It has to survive being wrong, so every varying line needs a version for the day nothing happened or something broke. And no aphoristic cadence: the short manufactured-contrast rebuttal is itself a listed tell, which means no run of sentences shaped like "not X, but Y" and no section landing on a neat inversion. Once in a document is fine, and repeated is the tell.
