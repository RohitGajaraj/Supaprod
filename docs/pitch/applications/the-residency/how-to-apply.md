## Before you start (10 minutes)

**1. Fill the five founder slots** in [`application.md`](./application.md):
- Favourite book, and one line on why
- Demo video link, or leave blank
- The employment clause in "how long have you been working on this"
- Roommates: yes or no
- How you heard about them, and who inspired you to apply

Of these, the **book** and **who inspired you** carry the most weight. This is a house, not a fund, and both questions are really asking what you are like.

**2. The commit count is deliberately absent from this application.** It contradicted the agent story (the number would be measuring them, not you) and reads as a vanity metric to a house full of engineers. The number that does appear is the shipped-feature count. Re-pull it with `bash scripts/dashboard-tally.sh`: 362 shipped of 401 specced on 2026-07-31.

**3. Decide the demo video.** The current cut is 4:51. That is too long for a first impression from a program that repeatedly asks you to be concise. **Leave the field blank rather than pasting it**, unless a short cut exists by then. They accept "n/a" and say so on the form.

## Filling it

The form has seven sections down the left rail: about you, your work, why this idea, progress, similar work, past programs, how you found us. `application.md` follows the same order, so work top to bottom.

**Already filled and correct on the form:** first name, last name, location, gender, the five residency locations, cofounder no, looking for a cofounder yes, referred by alumni no. Leave all of it.

### The one thing that will sink this application if you get it wrong

**No links in any prose answer.** Their header says it plainly and tells you it is being graded:

> _"part of what we're evaluating is how well you can explain your work without leaning on external references."_

Six fields ask for URLs and only those six get them: link to your work, demo video, github, linkedin, x/twitter, personal website. Everywhere else, the words carry it. Before you hit next on each section, scan the free-text boxes for `http` and delete anything you find.

### The second thing

Five questions say **"be concise"**. They mean it. If an answer runs past roughly eight lines on screen, cut it. Every draft in `application.md` is already trimmed, so the risk is you adding to them, not the other way around.

- No demo login goes to this program. Both spare logins (`meridian@`, `lantern@`) stay held for programmes that will actually review a product.

## Two things to know before you accept, if it comes to that

**The dates collide.** The cohort runs 7 September to 29 November. That overlaps the September public launch, and it overlaps the South Park Commons bootcamp (late September to late November) almost exactly. If both land, they cannot both happen, and SPC pays $400K where this pays nothing.

**This is not funding.** The Residency takes no equity, which is good, but it is paid co-living, which means it costs money rather than providing it. What you get is a room, a house full of people building, and a deadline. Apply because that is genuinely useful and it costs an hour, not because it solves the money problem.

## The honest read on our odds

**For us:** he is doing exactly what the program exists to support, right now, alone, and can prove it. The three-year accomplishment window happens to be the strongest three years he has. The contrarian-belief question suits someone with a real thesis.

**Against us:** The Residency's reputation is built on very early-career builders, often college-age. A founder with roughly a decade of industry experience and an MBA is not their typical resident. The answers deal with this by never raising it, and by opening the accomplishments answer on how much has changed rather than how much has accumulated. The register stays plain on purpose, because a corporate voice would confirm the concern instantly.
