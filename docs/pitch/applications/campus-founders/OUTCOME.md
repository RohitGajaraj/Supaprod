## No reason is given, and none may be invented

*"Teams that more closely match the specific criteria and objectives"* names **no criterion**. It is a fit statement, and the standing rule holds: **recording an invented reason is worse than recording none**, because a later session reads it as signal and moves the positioning against something nobody said.

**Do not read "teams" as a comment on solo founding.** It is ordinary startup usage for companies, it appears in template rejections everywhere, and treating a plural noun as a verdict is exactly the over-read this rule exists to prevent.

## But two facts are OURS, and both were on file before we sent it

### 1. They publish a structural bar we did not meet, and we recorded it as decisive

CF's stated criterion 3 is **"Team composition (technical + business founders)"** — at least one tech and one business co-founder, with a published exception for exceptional solo founders. **This was a solo application.**

Our own pre-filing documents say so, plainly:

| Where | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| [`APPLICATION-FINAL.md:213`](./APPLICATION-FINAL.md) | *"**This is the answer that decides this application.** CF's stated bar is 'at least one tech and one business co-founder' with exceptions for exceptional solo founders."* |
| [`FILL-SHEET-38-FIELDS.md:152`](./FILL-SHEET-38-FIELDS.md) | *"CF's bar is one tech plus one business co-founder; this is a solo application. A reviewer weighing an exception wants to see the person."* |
| [`FILL-SHEET-38-FIELDS.md:16`](./FILL-SHEET-38-FIELDS.md) | *"Team Intro Video ... **matters most here**, because CF's bar is two co-founders and this is a solo application"* |

**We applied for a published exception and did not get it.**

> **That is a fact about what we knew going in. It is NOT a claim about what they decided.** The distinction is the whole discipline: a recorded structural mismatch is evidence; "we lost on team" is an invented reason wearing evidence's clothes. The letter never says which criterion, and it may well have been market, stage or something we cannot see.

There was no pitch slot. **The screen ran on the written application and the deck**, four days before the decision date.

**That matters for what to fix.** The live form carries **no problem, market, defensibility or competitor questions at all** — the fill sheet records that discovery: *"None of those questions exist. They live in the required pitch deck."* So **the deck was the thing screened**, and rewriting the form answers in response to this outcome would be optimising against a document nobody weighed. Same lesson EF taught with *"progress to the next stage"*.

## The forward rule

> ### Mark an EXCEPTION APPLICATION as one, on the board, before filing.
>
> **When a programme publishes a structural bar we do not meet and we file against a stated exception, that fact belongs on the tracker card with its own expectation.**

Without it, a no gets read as a signal about the product — when the programme told us the odds before we started. **Three of the four rejections so far carry no product signal at all. This one has a known structural cause that was written down before filing, and the card did not say so.**

The `Solo founder` field on this board already has a `BLOCKED` option for a published two-founder rule. **CF is the in-between case it does not cover**: a bar with a published exception. That is not `BLOCKED` and it is not a clean `Yes`.

## What did NOT change

**The positioning.** Four rejections across four programmes with four different filters. The discipline recorded after the second stands: fix what we can prove is wrong, leave the thesis alone until something says it is wrong. **Nothing in this letter says it** — it does not name a criterion at all.
