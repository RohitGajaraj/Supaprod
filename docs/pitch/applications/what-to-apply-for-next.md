# What do we apply for next?

> _The standing answer. Created 2026-08-14. **Ask this question and read this file — do not re-derive the ranking.** It is regenerated by [`scripts/rank-funding-programmes.py`](../../../scripts/rank-funding-programmes.py) from the sweep, so re-run that after any page-state change rather than editing scores by hand._

## The priority order, in the founder's words

**Restated 2026-08-14 and binding. It replaces the earlier order, which put deadline and location first.**

1. **Money, and how much of it.** *"Priority for us is always whoever gives me money"*, ranked on *"how big is it."* **A larger cheque outside the US outranks a smaller one inside it.**
2. **Network access.**
3. **Credits.**
4. **Relocation to the US** — a preference now, no longer a constraint.
5. Everything else — brand, customer access, dilution, effort.

> ### ✅ Relocation is solved, and it changes the maths
>
> **Founder, 2026-08-14: *"I am ready to move in. I'm quitting the job, and I'm ready to move in."***
>
> An in-person programme anywhere is therefore **no longer a cost to score against**. Location dropped from a heavy weight to a light one and nothing scores below 5.5 on it, because an unfamiliar city is now a neutral fact rather than a penalty. **Money quantum does the ranking.** This is why Antler US, Afore and EF The Bridge sit at the top while Bangalore-convenient options no longer get an advantage for being close to home.

**Deadline is not on that list and is still an axis**, because a closing form is a forcing function rather than a preference. It breaks ties and it never outranks what is actually on offer.

**One axis is not negotiable and sits outside the order: realistic odds.** An application that cannot be won is worth nothing regardless of the prize, which is why a large fund we will not get into ranks below a small programme we will.

> **What changed when the order was restated.** The first ranking led on deadline and scored Bangalore-reachable highest, which optimised for convenience and against the destination. Money now leads and location is a light upside. **Tier 1 grew from 17 to 39.**
