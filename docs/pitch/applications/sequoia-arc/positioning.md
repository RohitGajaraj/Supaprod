# Sequoia Arc — what it selects for, and the angle in

> _Created: 2026-08-13 · **Deadline 2026-08-17, 11:59pm PT.** Verified two ways: the Notion board checked the site on 2026-07-31, and Sequoia's own posts put the bi-annual close on 17 August. Apply at `https://www.sequoiacap.com/arc/apply/`._

## What Arc actually is

**Not an accelerator in the YC sense.** A seven-week company-building immersion attached to a pre-seed or seed investment, run twice a year, open call. Terms are set per company rather than published, so there is no standard deal to prepare against. The perks stack (NVIDIA, Azure, Cloudflare credits) is real money and worth taking regardless.

**Solo is not a published blocker.** Arc has taken solo founders. Treat the cofounder question as one to answer well, never one to apologise for.

## The three things they screen on

| They look for | Our standing |
| --- | --- |
| **Founder-market fit, verified by reference calls** | **Strongest card, and it is checkable.** ISRO, then Infineon, then a senior AI product role at Intellect, a BFSI technology OEM. Close to a decade of being the person who had to defend a call. References will say the same thing the application says. |
| **A market changing on a dated trigger** | **Strong.** Build cost collapsed inside eighteen months. 82% of the market reports AI already makes them measurably more productive while burnout moved 44.7% to 55.7%. Speed is solved; the call is not. |
| **PMF archetype: Hard Tech, Urgent Need, or Consumption** | **Urgent Need**, and say so in their words. The pain exists today and compounds monthly as more work moves to agents. Do not claim Consumption; we have no usage curve. |

## The angle, and it is different from the YC one

**YC gets the wedge. Sequoia gets the asset.**

Sequoia's largest outcomes are systems of record. That is the frame their partners already think in, and it is the honest description of what this is: **the seat that decides what a company builds has no system of record, and a new version of that seat is being created right now.** Instagram replaced a roughly thirteen-person canonical team with pods of four to six engineers led by what Mosseri calls product staff, one person absorbing design, data and research (July 2026). One person is now accountable for calls that used to be split across five specialists.

**Then the durable asset, stated so it survives a query.** Not the record, because the record is backfillable and was rebuilt twice on the record. **The forecast: what a team believed would happen, frozen before the outcome was known.** It leaves no trace unless something caught it at the moment of the call, and we hold it immutable in the database rather than in a policy.

## What to concede, early and without hedging

Zero outside users. No revenue. Solo. **State all three in the traction answer rather than letting a partner find them**, because at pre-seed Sequoia is buying the insight and the person, and a founder who hides a zero is telling them something worse than the zero.

The one thing that replaces traction here: the product runs end to end and there is a live login. An artifact a partner can open beats any number we could offer.

## Three prohibitions carried from canon

1. **No present-tense claim of accumulated learning.** The loop is wired and proven and begins accruing on first real use.
2. **No unbroken signal to shipped to learned chain.** It breaks in two places.
3. **No inevitability language.** The most credentialed post of its era called web3 risky and inevitable.
