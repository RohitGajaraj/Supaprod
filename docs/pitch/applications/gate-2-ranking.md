## The ask — recommendation and comparables

**You have no stated ask anywhere, and every fund conversation needs one.** Accelerators set their own terms, so this is for the separate angel and pre-seed conversation.

### What the market is paying, from this sweep's own verified terms

| Programme | Cheque | Equity | Implied post-money |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character Labs | $200K | 5% | **$4.0M** |
| Solo Founders Program | $100K | 2.5% | **$4.0M** |
| Techstars | $220K | ~5% + MFN | ~$4.4M |
| Berkeley SkyDeck _(filed)_ | $210K | 7.25% | $2.9M |
| ERA | $150K | 6% | $2.5M |
| EF The Bridge | $125K | 8% + MFN | $1.6M |
| Antler UK | £125K | 8.5% | ~£1.5M |
| 100X.VC | ~$150K | 15% | ~$1.0M |
| Conviction Embed _(filed)_ | $250K | **uncapped MFN** | sets no price |

**The accelerator band for a pre-revenue solo founder is $2.5M–$4.4M post.** Those terms are deliberately below market — they are paying partly in programme value. Independent pre-seed for a technical solo founder with a **working end-to-end product** rather than a deck runs materially above it.

### The recommendation

**Lead with an uncapped post-money SAFE on MFN terms. Fall back to $1M at an $8M cap.**

**Why MFN first.** Conviction Embed is already filed at **$250K on an uncapped no-discount MFN SAFE**. If that lands it sets no price, and every later cheque inherits whatever the first priced round decides. Naming a number now can only cost you — it caps the upside and invites a negotiation you do not need to have while you have no revenue to defend a number with.

**Why $1M at $8M if pressed.** $8M is roughly twice the accelerator-implied band, which is the normal spread between programme terms and independent terms, and it is defensible on the build rather than on traction: a product that runs end to end, 5,172 commits in ten weeks, solo. $1M funds roughly eighteen months at your cost base through public launch and the first real users — the exact period where the answer to *"is anyone using it"* changes.

**What not to do.** Do not name a valuation on an accelerator form. Do not raise more than $1M pre-launch; a larger round now prices in traction you do not have yet and makes the next round harder, not easier.
