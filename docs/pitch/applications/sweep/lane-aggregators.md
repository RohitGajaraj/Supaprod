## 🟡 Blocked, and needs founder access

- **Iterative** — The homepage renders and describes the batch model ('We invest US $150K to $500K each in a batch of startups, twice a year') but the fetched content contained no application form, no application link and no statement of whether applications are currently open or closed. Nothing blocked access — the state is simply not published on the page fetched. · https://www.iterative.vc/
- **Neo Residency** — The homepage advertises Neo Residency but the only application state shown anywhere on the page is under Neo Scholars, which reads 'The application deadline has passed.' The Residency block says 'Get guaranteed admission to Neo Residency as a student team', which describes a student pathway rather than an open general application, and no separate Residency application link or deadline is published on the page. · https://neo.com/
- **a16z Speedrun (SR008)** — a16z.com/speedrun states only 'SR007 will take place in San Francisco from July 27th to Oct 11, 2026' — a batch already running — and directs to speedrun.a16z.com for more info. No SR008 application window, deadline or open/closed statement appears on the a16z page, and the speedrun.a16z.com subdomain was not fetched. · https://speedrun.a16z.com
- **Orange DAO Fellowship** — Fetch of https://www.orangedao.xyz/fellowship returned CRAWL_NOT_FOUND — the page could not be retrieved at all, so neither an open nor a closed state could be observed. Not a login or paywall; the URL itself may have moved. · https://www.orangedao.xyz/fellowship
- **Z Fellows** — The homepage renders and describes the programme, but https://zfellows.com/apply returned CRAWL_NOT_FOUND and the homepage content fetched contained no deadline, no open/closed statement and no visible application form. · https://zfellows.com/
- **Xpreneurs Incubator** — Fetch of https://www.xpreneurs.io/ returned CRAWL_LIVECRAWL_TIMEOUT — the site did not respond within the crawl window, so no open or closed state could be observed. · https://www.xpreneurs.io/
- **F6S Programs directory** — Both https://www.f6s.com/programs and https://www.f6s.com/accelerators returned an anti-bot interstitial: 'Checking your browser — We think you might be a bot. To regain access, please make sure that cookies and JavaScript are enabled before reloading the page.' The directory and every programme page behind it are inaccessible to automated fetching and require a real browser session. · https://www.f6s.com/programs
- **Vercel AI Accelerator** — The page shows a live 'Apply now' call to action with no closed notice, but a third-party 2026 resource list records the 2026 cohort as having closed on 2026-02-16. The page itself publishes no dates, no deadline and no cohort year, so the live 'Apply now' cannot be distinguished from an evergreen waitlist. State genuinely undetermined from the page. · https://vercel.com/ai-accelerator
- **Forum Ventures Accelerator** — The programme page renders in full and describes the offer, but the fetched content contained no application form, no deadline, no cohort dates and no statement of whether applications are currently open. Nothing blocked access; the state is simply not published on the page fetched. · https://www.forumvc.com/accelerator
- **Hook** — The site describes the programme and states 'You can apply as an individual, or as a startup', but the fetched content published no application form, no deadline and no open/closed statement for the current batch. Additionally the eligibility line 'Hook is designed for the most ambitious entrepreneurs from 42' suggests a possible affiliation gate to the 42 coding school that could not be confirmed or ruled out from the page. · https://hook.vc/
