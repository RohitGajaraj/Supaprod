## The single most important thing about this application

**SPC is anti-pitch, and this is the opposite register from the YC application.**

A past Founder Fellow's published advice: _"Don't pitch or tell them why you'd be a good founder fellow, show who you are."_ SPC's own framing is a community of people exploring at the frontier, built around the idea of the "-1 to 0" stage. Their partners interview conversationally, not adversarially.

The YC application was engineered for a skeptical partner scanning for formidability in ninety seconds. **Sending that voice here would actively hurt us.** It would read as a polished outsider performing at a community that selects for genuine curiosity.

**What changes in practice:**

- Fewer numbers, more thinking. Numbers still appear, but they support a story about how he works rather than carrying the argument alone.
- The discarded versions are an asset, not an embarrassment. SPC explicitly asks how you generate ideas, including concepts you threw away.
- Show range. The form literally invites several ideas, "including ones that are a bit out there," and says many successful SPC companies ended up on ideas the team did not start with. Listing only Supaprod would under-answer the question they actually asked.
- Write like a person. Contractions are fine. Certainty about the problem, honesty about the answer.

## Their published rubric: velocity plus acceleration

SPC's selection frame, from their own guidance:

**Velocity, what you have done:**
- Bias for building, specifically _"you ship things even when no one is paying you to"_
- Demonstrated leadership
- Deep domain expertise

**Acceleration, your forward trajectory:**
- High risk tolerance
- Intellectual curiosity across fields
- Emotional intelligence
- A specific ten-year ambition

**And the line that shapes the whole application:** _"Frontload the things you are doing now that you did not do two years ago."_

## Our one real risk here, and the fix

**Risk: we read as too far along, or as an outsider pitching a polished company at a community built for exploration.**

SPC's heritage is the "-1 to 0" stage. Supaprod is a working product with a September launch. The Fellowship branch is explicitly for people who "started a company and are ready to pitch and fundraise," so we are eligible, but the cultural centre of gravity is exploration.

**The fix, applied throughout the drafted answers:**
1. Be certain about the problem and genuinely open about the answer. The thesis is a decade old; the current product is one attempt at it, the fifth.
2. Lead the problem-space answer with the question, not the product.
3. Take their invitation to list adjacent and "out there" directions seriously. It shows he is still exploring, which is what this community is for.
4. Never use the investor-deck register. No "TAM", no "SOM", no "moat" as a noun.
