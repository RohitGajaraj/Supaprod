# Berkeley SkyDeck, Batch 23 — what it selects for, and the angle in

> _Created: 2026-08-13 · **Deadline 2026-08-21, 11:59pm PT** · Apply at `https://www.f6s.com/skydeck-batch-23/apply`_

## What SkyDeck actually is

**Berkeley-affiliated accelerator, not a residency or grant.** A traditional three-to-four month program with $210K investment, $7.5K program fee, and structured mentorship. Batch model, not rolling. Emphasis on technical founders, engineering-heavy product builds, and scalability. Open to solo founders globally.

## The three things they screen on

| They look for | Our standing |
| --- | --- |
| **Founder-market fit and technical depth** | **Strong.** Not a first-time PM; close to a decade in product plus a decade-long pattern of learning the engineering. ISRO to Infineon to BFSI OEM at scale. The codebase is solo-authored, reviewed and audited. |
| **A clear product-market pain with timing** | **Strong.** Agents commoditized building. Product decisions became the bottleneck. 82% of PMs already measure productivity gains from AI, but cannot defend the calls they make. Timing: the infrastructure layer gets decided in the next two quarters. |
| **Proof of execution** | **Strongest card.** Not wireframes or a slide deck. A working end-to-end system, 5,000+ commits in ten weeks, solo, running daily. Live at supaprod.ai with a login anyone can test. The product runs on itself. |

## The angle, and it is different from the YC and Sequoia ones

**YC gets the wedge. Sequoia gets the asset. Berkeley SkyDeck gets the thesis proved.**

SkyDeck counts founders who ship fast and think in systems. The frame their partners already use is: engineering rigor plus product insight equals durability. Supaprod is exactly that — **a solo founder with BFSI-grade governance experience built the agentic infrastructure layer that every AI-first company will need, and it works end to end.** Not a prototype, not a wireframe. The thing runs, it has an audit trail, and the person directing it proved the core claim of the product.

The durability here is not the record — that is backfillable, and it has been rebuilt twice. **The durability is the forecast captured at the moment of decision, before the outcome was known.** It leaves no trace without something that caught it in real time. That is the thing nobody else has built, and it is the thing everyone will need.

## What to concede, early and without hedging

Zero outside users. No revenue. Solo. State these as fact, not apology. SkyDeck takes founders who ship hard proofs instead of big claims. This is a better signal to them than traction numbers would be, because the signal is credibility.

The one thing that replaces traction here: the product works end to end and runs on itself. An artifact on the shelf of your laptop beats any metric we could offer, because SkyDeck is engineering-first.

## Three anchors carried from canon

1. **The build story is rare.** Agents write the code. Directing and reviewing 5,000+ commits in ten weeks is itself the hard thing. State it that way.
2. **The founder is the thesis.** Supaprod claims one person directing agents can match a team. The person exists. The product exists. That is a check that most founders cannot mark.
3. **This founder thinks in systems.** ISRO (hardware that launches once, no second chance). Infineon (supply chains and semiconductor margins). Intellect (200+ financial institutions, 70+ countries, regulated). Not a PM who read about B2B. A PM who lived in rooms where governance was not optional.
