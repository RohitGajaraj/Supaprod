## The one-line lesson from the failed week

Every real English dictionary word — including genuinely obscure ones (weft, pawl, reeve, aplomb, engram, takt, kilter, fettle...) — is already registered on `.com`, `.ai`, and `.io`. ~290 real words were checked tonight; effectively zero bare-word survivors. The ownable space in 2026 is (a) borrowed words from smaller languages, (b) clean coins with real semantic roots, (c) rare surnames. That is where this shortlist comes from.

## Recommendation: **Taktur** (`taktur.ai`)

**Icelandic for "rhythm, beat" — the living descendant of _takt_, as in takt time, the lean-manufacturing term for production cadence.** It is Cadence's direct heir without the trademark: same meaning, new word. TAK-tur, 6 letters, 2 syllables, spellable after one hearing, `-ur` ending in the Cursor shape family.

- `taktur.ai` — **unregistered** (registry-confirmed 2026-07-14, twice)
- `taktur.com` — parked, listed for sale on GoDaddy (defensive/upgrade acquisition path)
- Exact string unclaimed in software anywhere: the only exact-name entities on earth are a Kentucky Icelandic-horse farm, a tiny opaque Icelandic ehf, and a small Spotify techno act
- Languages: clean in ES/PT/FR/DE/JA/ZH/AR/TR; Hindi near-word "Thakur" is an honorific (neutral-positive)
- Honest risks: the `takt-` neighborhood in software is crowded — **Taktile** (YC, $110M, AI decisioning for fintech) and **Takt.io** (AI warehouse labor SaaS) are the reference marks a trademark attorney must clear in class 9/42. Coexistence looks like the norm (the root is diluted), and "taktur ai" is an uncontested search query, but do the knockout search before filing.

The rename story writes itself: _"Cadence became Taktur — same heartbeat, new name."_
