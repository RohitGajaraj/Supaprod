## 2. Why rename: the Cadence baseline (scored 3/10)

The rename was driven by ownability, and the baseline research proved the instinct right. "Cadence" was vetted exactly like a candidate:

- **`cadence.ai` is Cadence Design Systems' front door for agentic AI.** The domain (registered 2017) 301-redirects to `https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/ai/overview.html` — their "Cadence.AI" marketing, which brands the company as "leading the agentic AI transformation" (AgentStack / ChipStack agentic platforms). Cadence Design Systems is a **$102.7B market-cap** EDA/software company using a brand-protection registrar. A startup selling agentic AI software under the name Cadence collides head-on with a $100B company's active AI branding.
- **`cadence.com`** — held by Cadence Design Systems via CSC Corporate Domains **since 1989-04-14**, with server delete/transfer/update prohibitions. Unobtainable.
- **`cadence.io`** — registered 2013, transferred 2024, parked dark by an undisclosed holder.
- **`getcadence.ai`** — registered 2025-08-08 by a privacy-shielded registrant; live "Launching Soon" email-capture page. Another product is about to launch on the compromise domain.
- **Cadence (Cadence Solutions, digital health)** — raised **$100M in June 2026 at a $1.23B valuation** (Spark Capital; STAT News coverage). It owns the "Cadence AI startup" press mindshare.
- **Cadence Workflow (Uber / CNCF)** — open-source distributed workflow orchestration used by Uber, DoorDash, Coinbase. The exact engineers and founders Cadence-the-product targets already know "Cadence" as an orchestration engine — nearly the same concept space.
- **Genericized vocabulary** — David Sacks' "The Cadence" essay plus "meeting cadence" / "release cadence" make the word common-noun vocabulary in the exact PM/founder audience; Crunchbase lists many more Cadences (bank, running coach, events, fintech).

**Baseline verdict:** a lovely, premium, on-story word the startup can never own. Radio test passes on sound and fails on destination — a listener who hears the name lands on Cadence Design Systems' website. **Score 3/10. Any serious rename candidate only had to beat a 3.**

## 3. Methodology (read before trusting any single fact)

- **Five background research rounds in one session (2026-07-16), ~30 agents, ~680 live checks**: authoritative registry RDAP/whois lookups (Verisign, Identity Digital, Google Registry, nic.ai, nic.so), live-site fetches to classify parked vs live vs for-sale, USPTO checks (live `tmsearch.uspto.gov` query where possible, Justia/uspto.report mirrors elsewhere), funding-news and company verification via web search.
- Every finalist got both a **sweep** (domains, social handles, trademark quick-scan) and **adversarial verification** (a dedicated agent instructed to kill the name). The adversarial step earned its cost twice: it killed round-1 winner Emberloop and exposed Supaduct's literal-pipes problem.
- **Domain statuses are point-in-time snapshots of 2026-07-16 and decay fast.** Real examples from this research: `getemberloop.com` was registered by a stranger **4 days** before we checked (a live SaaS now runs on it); `supaduck.com` went **3 weeks** before we checked. Re-verify before acting on any "available" below.
- Trademark findings are **web-level scans, not legal clearance**. Where a primary database was bot-walled (EUIPO/TMview, some USPTO endpoints), that is stated explicitly. An attorney knockout search is a standing condition of the decision (§6).

## 5. Round-by-round evidence

### Round 1 — the founder's seven + the Cadence baseline + fresh alternatives (9 agents)

#### 5.1 Dialogue (typed "Dialouge.ai") — 2/10, fatal

- **Domains:** `dialouge.ai` (the typo) free; `dialogue.ai` = GoDaddy premium-sale lander (registered 2018-04-21, Spaceship); `dialogue.com` = brokered by **Hilco Digital Assets** ("serious inquiries only" — six-to-seven-figure tier; registered 1999).
- **Fatal conflict:** **Dialogue AI** (`dialogueai.com`) — founded 2025, **$6M seed led by Lightspeed (Oct 2025)** with Seven Stars, Uncommon Projects, Tornante; angels include Discord's CPO and ex-Nextdoor CPO. Customers: Wayfair, Square (Block), Nextdoor, Suno. Product: AI-moderated live customer-research interviews — **sold to the exact same buyer (PMs/product teams)** under the identical name.
- Also: Dialogue Health (Canadian telehealth, acquired by Sun Life for C$365M), Google Dialogflow (anchors "dialog(ue)" to chatbots), Dialpad, Dialog (AI shopping agents, $4.4M seed).
- **Radio test fails three ways:** dialogue vs dialog vs the founder's own live typo "dialouge."
- **Story mis-signal:** "dialogue systems" is the legacy academic term for chatbots — the name markets the least differentiated part of the product.

**Rescue attempt (round 3, because it was the founder's #1):** verdict **fatal, triple-blocked**:
1. **Trademark:** a live USPTO `tmsearch` query (2026-07-16) found **472 DIALOGUE-formative records, 32 LIVE in classes 9/42**, including **Pheon, Inc.'s REGISTERED standalone DIALOGUE word mark (SN 98680821, IC 009 + 042)** for its AI companion-chat app (`dialogue.app`). A new DIALOGUE filing for AI software faces near-certain §2(d) refusal.
2. **Market:** Dialogue AI verified alive and growing mid-2026 (14 employees per Tracxn, May 2026; no pivot/rename). No coexistence framing survives — Cadence's own product *runs discovery* for product teams.
3. **Domain:** `dialogue.ai` is offer-only; comparable one-word .ai sales 2024–26: fin.ai $1M, you.ai $700k, mini.ai $100k → realistic $100k–$400k, with Dialogue AI itself as the natural rival bidder.
4. **The "Dialouge" intentional-misspelling route fails on research:** successful misspelled brands are phonetic respellings (Lyft, Flickr, Tumblr); a mid-word transposition typo reads as an error, autocorrects into the competitor's name, and peer-reviewed work (Ohio State "Badd Choyce"; Notre Dame Lyft-vs-Lift) finds unconventional spellings reduce trust unless heavily ad-subsidized.

#### 5.2 Orientale — 1.5/10, fatal

- **Domains:** `orientale.ai` hand-registrable (free *because* the name is radioactive); `orientale.com` Afternic for-sale lander (registered 1997).
- **The blocker is the word:** "Oriental" applied to people is flagged dated/offensive in US English (Merriam-Webster, OED); the US removed the word from federal law in **2016**; the perfume industry formally retired the "Oriental" category in 2021. "Orientale" is transparently the same root with a feminine -e — a US-first launch spends its first impression explaining the name.
- Also: Orients.Ai (live, one edit away), ORIENTATE AI LTD (UK, 2024), Orientale HK fashion brand; the word's actual associations are cuisine, perfume, rugs, belly dance — zero story fit.
- **Radio test fails:** most listeners type `oriental.ai`; pronunciation unsettled across or-ee-en-TAL / -TAH-lay / -TAHL.

#### 5.3 Galilei (typed "Galiliei.ai") — 1.5/10, fatal

- **Domains:** `galiliei.ai` (double typo) free; `galilei.ai` registered-parked (GoDaddy, 2023 — buyout required); `galileo.ai` owned via MarkMonitor by a funded company.
- **Fatal namespace:** four established "Galileo" tech brands: **Galileo** (`galileo.ai` — AI evals/observability, $45M Series B Oct 2024, $68M+ total; HP, Comcast, Twilio), **LogRocket's Galileo AI** (AI product analyst marketed explicitly to product managers — the exact buyer), **Galileo AI** (text-to-UI, $15M raised, acquired by Google mid-2025 → Stitch), **Galileo Financial Technologies** (SoFi). Plus Galilei Software GmbH (exact spelling, German clinical software) and the EU Galileo GNSS / Travelport Galileo saturating search.

#### 5.4 ProductMatter (typed "product matter.ai") — 3/10 → deep-dive 5.5/10, usable with conditions (not chosen)

- **Domains (best hand on the founder's original list):** `productmatter.ai` AND `productmatters.ai` both unregistered; `productmatter.com` = **$20,000 Afternic buy-now** (GoDaddy, registered 2013, expiry 2026-08-02 — investor-held; the deep-dive found GoDaddy's captive expiry pipeline means <15–20% odds of catching a drop; for-sale landers almost always auto-renew); `productmatters.com` Dutch-parked since 2002.
- **Primary risk — JetBrains "Matter":** launched Oct 2025 as "the AI development companion for **product teams**" (PMs prototype in the codebase without coding, GitHub PRs); still EAP mid-2026 but riding JetBrains' July 2026 "AI for Teams and Organizations" push (InfoWorld, The New Stack coverage). "ProductMatter" wholly contains MATTER plus a descriptive prefix, and their tagline literally describes the compound — genuine §2(d) exposure **if** JetBrains has filed MATTER in classes 9/42 (unverified; the gating fact for this name). Also: Productboard's registered "MAKE WHAT MATTERS" (Reg 7575960) in the same class; the Amazon/Apple/Google Matter smart-home standard owns the word in search; Matters.AI ($6.25M, data security); matterapp.com (funded feedback SaaS).

- **Descriptiveness:** probably survives §2(e)(1) as suggestive (a "mental leap" is required); the real refusal risk is 2(d), not descriptiveness. Expect a "PRODUCT" disclaimer.
- **Radio test:** singular/plural coin-flip (matter/matters) — ~80–90% mitigated by owning both .ai forms, but the hesitation moment persists in speech; social handles can only be one of them.
- **Brand ceiling:** Product+noun compounds read Productboard-tier, not Linear-tier; research found experienced founders choose evocative over descriptive names. It positions the product next to the tools it claims to sit above.

## 6. The winner: why Supaprod

**The vote: 2–1.** Sweep: 8.5/10, "register this week." Kill-attempt: reject. Risk tie-breaker: "manageable, can use" with the four conditions. The founder reviewed the dissent explicitly and locked the name.

**Why it beat everything else:**
- **Best domain hand of all 19 names** — the only candidate with both `.com` and `.ai` unregistered at standard fee and no fatal conflict attached (Supaduct matched the domains but *is* the name of pipes; Stokehold matched the cleanliness but failed the founder's taste).
- **The US trademark path looks the cleanest of the finalists** — zero SUPAPROD usage anywhere, no US SUPERPROD registration found, crowded-SUPER-formative field cutting in favor of a distinctive compound.
- **The name is true twice** — product (the domain) and ships-to-prod (the engine) — in the vocabulary of both halves of the audience.
- **Its taxes are known, priced, and mitigable** — versus alternatives whose taxes were structural (Dialogue's registered-mark block, OrangeLoop's opposer, Supaduct's semantics).

**The three accepted taxes:**
1. **Variant handles forever** — `@supaprodhq` on X/Instagram/YouTube (GitHub and LinkedIn get the exact name). Standard startup pattern (Linear ran as @linear_app for years).
2. **The spoken leak lands somewhere real** — "supaprod" said aloud = "superprod" = a $120M French animation group's search namespace. Mitigated by defensive redirects (parked, see budget ruling), SERP capture at launch, and the "with an A" spoken rule.
3. **Supabase-school naming, permanently** — softened by the Supaglue precedent, Supabase's non-enforcement posture, and "prod" pointing at our own domain. The company will be "the second-most-famous supa- company in its investors' heads" until it earns otherwise.

**Perspective from five rounds:** *no* name scored clean — full ownability is a myth in the 2026 namespace. Every candidate carried taxes; the decision was never "find the taxless name" but "pick the taxes you can pay forever." Supaprod's are the most manageable set attached to the strongest asset position.
