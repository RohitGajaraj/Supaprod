# The demo video — Maya's Monday (read-aloud script)

> **Created 2026-07-27. Founder rulings: no live build, no full lifecycle, under three minutes, and open on a
> person rather than a product.** Every frame below was opened on the live site as `harbor@` tonight, so
> nothing here is written from the database or from hope.
>
> **309 spoken words across 20 steps: 2:12 at a measured 140 wpm, 2:03 at 150, and about 2:29 to 2:38 once you
> add the eight screen changes.** That is right on the 2:30 ceiling, so **pace matters more than any single
> word.** The first draft ran 430 words (3:18) and the landing page plus sign-in added two more screens, so
> there is no slack left. Do not add sentences back.
>
> **If you are running long, cut these two, in this order:** the second half of step 18 ("This is not storage.
> Next time someone proposes that bet...") saves 8 seconds, and the sources list in step 8 ("tickets, reviews,
> NPS, sales calls, Slack, the funnel" → "everything she already has") saves 5.
>
> The spine is the investor canon: **01 tells her what to build** → **02 runs the lifecycle** → **03 remembers
> and guides**. Door, body, brain. The difference from the earlier draft is that Maya carries it, and the
> layers are things that happen *to her*, not features in a list.
>
> Sibling cuts: the eight-beat single-bet journey is [`demo-video-one-journey.md`](./demo-video-one-journey.md);
> the eleven-step tour is [`video-scripts.md`](./video-scripts.md). **This is the one to shoot.**

## Before you roll (5 minutes, not optional)

1. **1920 x 1080.** Not 1440: the Spine clips after `06 Ship` and the one-loop frame dies.
2. Clean Chrome profile, one tab, no bookmarks bar. **Quit Slack and Mail.**
3. Sign in at `supaprod.ai/login` as `harbor@supaprod.ai` / `Supaprod!Harbor2026`. Confirm the avatar reads
   **Maya Ruiz** — the name on screen has to match the name in your voice.
4. **Sign out first** (so step 3 is a real sign-in), then sign back in once to prime the session and **dismiss two banners:** "New here? Take a 20-second tour" (**Not now**), and the upgrade nudge on
   `/brain` (its **x**). Reload. Confirm both stay gone.
5. Open all six URLs once, in order, so nothing cold-loads on camera.
6. **Never press a number key.** `1` approves and dispatches a real agent run. Navigate by URL only.
7. **Never open the account menu.** It prints `harbor@supaprod.ai`, and Maya's story dies on the spot.

**The eight screens, in order:**

```text
1  https://supaprod.ai                                        <- the public landing page
2  (click "Sign in", log in as harbor@supaprod.ai)
3  https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay
4  https://supaprod.ai/discover
5  https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=decide
6  https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=plan     <- then CLICK the checkout tab
7  https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=build
8  https://supaprod.ai/brain?tab=learnings
```

**Sign out of the recording profile before you start**, so step 3 is a real sign-in rather than a redirect.
Leave the browser's saved password in place so the form fills fast and the login frame stays short.

# THE RUN SHEET

**20 steps. `DO` is your hands, `SAY` is your mouth.** Never do both at once: finish the move, then talk over
the still frame. That is what makes it look calm instead of hurried.

Start recording, then wait two seconds of silence before step 1.

---

### SCREEN 1 · `https://supaprod.ai` — the public landing page

**1. DO** — Loaded before you hit record, scrolled to the very top. Touch nothing for **3 seconds**.
**[In frame: the hero "Supaprod tells you what to build." / "then builds it. ships it. grades it. gets
sharper.", and the three-column strip under it: `TO DECIDE WHAT TO BUILD · TO SHIP WHILE IT MATTERS · TO KNOW
IF YOU WERE RIGHT`.]**

**2. SAY**
> "This is Supaprod. Agents that know what to build, ship it, and remember.
>
> Three minutes will not cover a whole product lifecycle, so here are the three layers."

**WHY** Your hero already states the three layers in three columns. Reading them off your own marketing page
makes everything after it a proof of what the page claims, which is the strongest shape available. It also
puts the honest caveat up front, in your voice, before anyone can feel oversold.

---

### SCREEN 2 · sign in

**3. DO** — Click **"Sign in"** top-right. Sign in as `harbor@supaprod.ai`. Keep it brief, about **4 seconds**
on screen.

**4. SAY** (say it while the login page is up, then go quiet as the app loads)
> "Let me sign in to a live workspace. Nothing here is a mockup."

> ⚠️ **Do not name Maya yet.** The email field autofills **`harbor@supaprod.ai`**, and `/login` does not skip
> even with an active session — I verified both tonight. If you have already said "this is Maya", the viewer
> reads a different name two seconds later and the story breaks. She arrives at step 6, once you are inside.
> Framed this way nothing is fudged: you are signing in, as yourself, to a demo workspace.
>
> **Never click the avatar or account menu after this.** It prints the address again.

---

### SCREEN 3 · `https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay`

**5. DO** — You land here after signing in. Let it settle **2 seconds**. Then move the cursor slowly to the
headline **"21 calls wait on you"**, and up to the **Spine strip** across the very top (`01 Discover ·
02 Decide · 03 Plan · 04 Design · 05 Build · 06 Ship · 07 Learn`). Do not click either.

**6. SAY**
> "This is Maya. She runs product on Relay, a consumer app for homeowners.
>
> This is her Monday: twenty-one calls waiting, and her whole loop on one screen. Discover, decide, plan,
> design, build, ship, learn."

**WHY** Now the person arrives, in her own workspace, with her name on the avatar. The Spine does double duty:
it proves the whole lifecycle exists now, so you never have to tour it later.

---

### SCREEN 4 · `https://supaprod.ai/discover`

**7. DO** — Go to the URL. Wait for it to paint. Do **not** scroll yet. The signal cards are in the middle
column; each shows a source chip, a quote, a small line reading `→ THEME NAME · N SIGNALS`, and a code like
`SIG·DC47C1`.

**8. SAY**
> "Layer one tells her what to build. It reads what she already has: tickets, reviews, NPS, sales calls,
> Slack, the funnel. Every line stays a verbatim quote with its source."

**9. DO** — **Scroll down slowly**, about two turns of the wheel, until the heading **"Clustered into bets"**
comes into frame with the ranked list under it. **Stop when rank `#4` is centred**: "Checkout and notification
friction in the homeowner app · 9 signals · 8 sources". Do not scroll past it — the empty "Market watch" block
is directly below.

**10. SAY**
> "Then it clusters them. This one is checkout friction: nine signals, eight different sources."

---

### SCREEN 5 · `https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=decide`

**11. DO** — Go to the URL. **Scroll down slowly** to the bet titled **"One-tap crypto checkout for add-ons"**.
Stop with it centred so its `killed` chip, its `ICE 4.0` score and the Critic verdict are all in frame.

**12. SAY**
> "And it argues with her. The exciting bet was one-tap crypto checkout. Everyone assumed the payment step was
> broken. It disagreed, called it an address problem, scored it a four and killed it.
>
> That reasoning is still on the record."

### SCREEN 6 · `https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=plan`

**13. DO** — Go to the URL. ⚠️ **It opens on the wrong spec.** Two tabs sit above the spec body. **Click the
second one, "Simplify checkout in the homeowner app".** Wait for the body to swap, then scroll a little so the
**"Outcome contract"** block is in frame.

**14. SAY**
> "Layer two runs the lifecycle. Her call becomes a spec she did not write, committing to a number before

### SCREEN 7 · `https://supaprod.ai/helio-labs-harbor/relay?stage=build`

**15. DO** — Go to the URL. Let the **"Files changed"** rail sit in frame: `+17 −2 across 4 files` and the four
paths. Move the cursor down to rest on the last one, the **migration** (`supabase/migrations/
20260719_address_last_confirmed.sql`). Do not click it.

**16. SAY**
> "Approved means built. Four files, their own branch, checks green.
>
> And the migration tracks the address a homeowner last confirmed, so an unchanged address skips the step.
> That is the NPS complaint, turned into a database column."

### SCREEN 8 · `https://supaprod.ai/brain?tab=learnings`

**17. DO** — Go to the URL. **Scroll down one turn** so the stat strip at the top (the one reading
`100% VALIDATED · +0 ICE MOVED`) goes **off the top of the frame**. Stop when a **`REVISE`** row sits directly
above a **`VALIDATED`** row, both fully visible.

**18. SAY**
> "Layer three is the brain. Completed checkouts went from fifty-nine percent to seventy-eight.
>
> And the same release on tablets barely moved, so the row above the win says needs revision. Nobody rounded
> it up.
>
> This is not storage. Next time someone proposes that bet, it warns her before she repeats it."

### CLOSE

**19. DO** — Stay exactly where you are. Do not move the mouse.

**20. SAY**
> "Signal, to shipped, to remembered. Agents do the work, Maya answers for it.
>
> There is a lot I have not shown you. Go and break it yourself."

**Then hold still and silent for 2 seconds before you stop recording.** A clean tail is what lets you trim
without clipping your last word.

## Why it is built this way (skip if you are about to shoot)

- **Step 1 to 4 buy you the viewer.** A person, her product, her Monday, before a single feature. The Spine in
  step 3 does double duty: it proves the whole lifecycle exists now, so you never have to tour it later.
- **Steps 9 and 10 are the wedge.** A chatbot agrees with you. This one told her no and showed its work. It is
  the moment the viewer stops filing this under "another AI tool".
- **Step 13 pays off step 6.** A customer sentence at 0:30 becomes a line of database schema at 1:30. That
  chain is the most concrete thing in the film.
- **Step 15 is the trust moment.** Skeptics judge the error path. Showing the miss directly above the win is
  what makes the seventy-eight percent believable.

# Traps that will cost you the take

- ⛔ **Never press a number key.** `1` approves and dispatches a real run, irreversibly, on camera.
- ⛔ **On `/discover`, never click the `⋯` menus** ("Theme actions", "Ask Supaprod to investigate"). They
  promote, draft specs and start missions. All real writes.
- ⛔ **On `?stage=plan`, do not click "Design this" or "Build this feature"** at the bottom of the spec.
- **`?stage=plan` opens on the WRONG spec.** Click the checkout tab or you narrate over a different bet.
- **On `/discover`, end the scroll on rank #4.** "Market watch" sits right below and is completely empty.
- **On `/brain?tab=learnings`, scroll the stat strip off the top.** It reads `+0 ICE MOVED`, which contradicts
  the closing line.
- **`/discover` has a different left nav** from every other screen. Cut in and out cleanly; do not pan it.
- If anything breaks: **stop, touch nothing, reload, re-take.** Nothing in this cut writes, so retakes are
  free. Harbor holds about 4,750 credits.

# Encode before uploading

```bash
ffmpeg -i take.mov -vf "scale=1920:-2" -c:v libx264 -preset slow -profile:v high \
  -pix_fmt yuv420p -b:v 3300k -maxrate 4200k -bufsize 8400k \
  -c:a aac -b:a 128k -ac 1 -movflags +faststart demo.mp4
```

About 55 MB at 2:25, comfortably under the 100 MB cap. H.264, not HEVC. **Scroll slowly** — fast scrolling is
what smears small text at this bitrate.
