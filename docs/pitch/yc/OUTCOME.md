## No reason is given, and none may be invented

**YC states as policy that it cannot give individual feedback, and it did not.** *"So many strong
submissions"* and *"turning away many promising companies"* are volume statements — the same shape
as EF's *"highly competitive"* and South Park Commons' *"not the right fit"*. The standing rule
holds: **recording an invented reason is worse than recording none**, because a later session reads
it as signal and moves the positioning against something nobody said.

**This is the fourth of five rejections that carries no product signal at all.** Only Hub71 named
criteria, and even that named them about the shortlisted startups rather than about us.

## What the letter does carry: reapplication is quantified

Every rejection so far has invited a future application. **YC is the first to put a number on it** —
*"over 50% of the startups we accept are repeat applicants"*, and applying again does not count
against you. That is a published statistic about YC's own funnel, not an assessment of this filing,
and it makes a second application an ordinary move rather than a fallback.

**A reapplication is a fresh application, not an edit of this one.** The five editable update
surfaces belonged to a live application and no longer apply.
