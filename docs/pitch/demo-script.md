# The demo script — evidence on screen, the loop closing live

> _Last updated: 2026-07-10. The doctrine first, then the walkthrough. Rehearse on a fresh production account twice before any showing (PC-27 acceptance). Timebox: 8 minutes core + Q&A._

## The doctrine (why our demo is different)

1. **Show the loop closing, never narrate it.** Their demo says "look what the AI did." Ours says "look what we can prove." Every beat ends on an audit-trail entry, not a claim.
2. **Rehearse the wrong path on purpose.** The single highest-trust moment (the research is unambiguous: skeptics judge the error path): let the Critic tear down OUR bet, show a revert, show a recorded miss. A demo that only wins looks like a demo; a demo that survives being wrong looks like a product.
3. **Live account, live data, no slides.** The zero-auth demo workspace (PC-04) is the fallback; the founder's dogfood workspace is the primary — its calibration line ("Supaprod called N of the last M") is real.
4. **Every claim spoken = wired.** The claim-on-wiring registry is one-pager.md's PROVEN/WIRING/ROADMAP tags — never speak a WIRING line as present tense.

## The 8-minute walkthrough

**Beat 0 — the cold open (30s).** A question, not a feature: _"Ask your team 'why did we decide X' about anything you shipped last quarter. That excavation — hours of Slack archaeology — is the job today."_ Type it into Supaprod: the answer in seconds, with the evidence chain. [the wedge, in the community's own words]

**Beat 1 — while you slept (60s).** Today. The judgment lane: three calls that need a human, ranked by consequence. The brief, bylined by the agents. The evidence strip: what the fleet did overnight — signals swept, themes clustered, one bet re-ranked _because an outcome landed_. Point at the byline: **who** did it, **what** they did, the evidence link. _"Nothing on this screen is a claim. Everything is a link to proof."_

**Beat 2 — the teardown (90s).** Pick the pet bet (theirs if live-demoing to a team — brave, decisive; ours if cold). Run the Critic: evidence-backed red-team with precedent — _"the last time this premise shipped, here's what happened."_ This is judgment with the evidence, not a chatbot's opinion.

**Beat 3 — decision to PR (2m).** Accept the reshaped bet → the spec generates from the record (drift-stamped projection, not a hand-written artifact) → acceptance criteria compile to checks → a mission dispatches → **a real PR opens with CI running** — under a merge gate no agent can bypass. Show the trace: every step, every tool call, every approval.

**Beat 4 — the wrong path (90s, the trust moment).** Take an agent-drafted change and **rewind it in one key** — the revert lands in the track record as its own evidence. Then the calibration surface: _"Supaprod called N of the last M — including the misses. We publish our error rate. Ask the last AI tool you bought for theirs."_

**Beat 5 — the compounding close (60s).** Outcomes (P-103, A-QUEUE.md: the door is Outcomes, not Brain): what this workspace has learned — decisions, outcomes, the ranking visibly informed by the record (_"your last three bets in this area under-performed; this one mirrors #2"_). Close: **"Agents do the work. You answer for it. Supaprod is how you answer — and it gets smarter about YOUR product with every outcome it records."**

## Audience variants

- **Investors:** lead Beat 5's mechanism (the compounding record = the moat), spend the saved time on the live DB numbers + the register-survived-audit story. Have the market one-liners ready (build commoditized; PM:eng inverting; YC's own RFS asks for this).
- **A team demo:** let THEM pick the bet for Beat 2 and answer Beat 0's question about THEIR product (connect one source live — the trust card at connect is part of the show).
- **Show HN (written form):** Beat 0 as the title's promise; a no-signup demo link (PC-04); the failure path as a GIF; the honest-limitations list in the post body; founder in the comments. Never influencer-voiced. [the listing strategy]

## Don'ts

No slides in the core. No "imagine if" sentences. No feature tour (the surfaces show up only as the loop passes through them). Never claim the self-improvement loop publicly until RPT-50 runs (Layer-1 workspace learning is the claimable line). If something breaks live: _that's Beat 4 happening for real — revert it, show the evidence, keep going._ The failure path is rehearsed precisely so a live failure is a feature.
