> _Created: 2026-07-12. Part of the GTM launch operating manual (see [README.md](./README.md)). Timeline: assets built days 0-2, wave fires days 3-7. The Show HN and Product Hunt cards are RESERVED for the listing week (v13 phase 3): nothing here touches them._
>
> **Standing rules (binding, every idea below inherits them):** everything ethical and honest. No fake scarcity, no astroturfing, no fabricated numbers, no disparaging named individuals. Claims follow PROVEN / WIRING / ROADMAP discipline ([one-pager](../pitch/one-pager.md)). The phrase "AI PM tool" never appears in our copy. The founder approves every outward send (v13 plan §4). All public copy follows [humanized-output](../conventions/humanized-output.md): no em/en dashes, no AI-cliche phrasing, sharp plain-words PM voice.

## 1. What "viral" means for us (so we aim the right weapon)

We are not chasing consumer-scale virality. The winning outcome of this wave is: **thousands of high-fit waitlist signups, 25+ design-partner candidates, and the startup ecosystem talking about the track record.** Every idea below is scored on its ability to convert attention into (a) waitlist signups and (b) design-partner conversations, not raw impressions.

The three assets no competitor can copy this week, which every idea exploits:

1. **The product built part of itself** (a real PR merged through its own gated path) — [PROVEN].
2. **The Critic teardown** (an agent that red-teams product bets with precedent) — [PROVEN], and it produces inherently shareable artifacts.
3. **The Track record** (every agent action lands in the audit trail, including the wrong calls) — [PROVEN], and radical honesty is the one launch posture AI-fatigued audiences still trust.

## 2. Scoring model

- **Reach** 1-5: realistic ceiling of qualified attention (5 = ecosystem-wide conversation)
- **Cost**: $ out of pocket (most are $0; HyperAgent GTM credit covers research ops per v13 plan §6)
- **Effort**: S (<0.5 day) / M (0.5-1.5 days) / L (2+ days)
- **Risk**: reputational or platform risk, low/med/high
- **P(hit)**: probability the idea meaningfully moves waitlist/design-partner numbers
- **EV**: judgment call combining the above, drives the rank

## 4. Top-5 execution briefs

### Brief 1 — The Launch Track record (the spine stunt)

**The idea.** The launch itself runs as a Supaprod mission on a public page. Every launch decision (which channel, which copy variant, what we killed, what flopped) is recorded as a real decision with evidence, and outcomes land against them during the week. Visitors watch a product org run by agents, live, on the most credible subject possible: our own launch. The meta is the message: we don't describe the product, we let people watch it work.

**Why it can pop.** Nobody can copy it (it requires the product), it produces fresh content every day of the wave for free, and it converts the strongest skeptic objection ("show me it actually working") into the landing page itself. Every other post in the wave links back to it, so attention compounds instead of evaporating.

**Execution.**

1. Day 0-1: create the "Launch Supaprod publicly" mission in the founder workspace. Seed it with the real launch decisions already made (channel ranking, copy picks, the 7-day plan itself).
2. Day 1-2: build the public read-only page (reuse the Track record share-link surface, [PROVEN]; a public route with workspace-scoped read-only data). Waitlist CTA pinned top and bottom: "This is your product org in 90 days. Join the waitlist."
3. Day 2: founder reviews every visible entry (nothing confidential, nothing about named third parties).
4. Day 3: goes live with the evidence drop (Brief 3 links here).
5. Days 3-7: every decision the wave forces (kill a channel, change a headline) gets recorded same-day. Wrong calls stay visible with their revert evidence. That honesty IS the show.

**Assets needed:** public share route (exists as Track record share link; verify scope), the mission seeded with real entries, waitlist embed, OG image ("Watch our launch decide itself").

**Success metric:** 500+ unique visitors by day 5; waitlist conversion from the page ≥8%; at least 3 unprompted shares of the page by non-followers.

**Kill criteria:** if by day 5 the page converts under 2% and drives no shares, stop investing in daily curation; keep the page as proof collateral for the HN week.

### Brief 2 — The Decision Autopsy engine (content series + waitlist mechanic, one system)

**The idea.** Two coupled parts. (a) **Public autopsies:** the Critic tears down famous, publicly documented product decisions using only public evidence (launch coverage, changelogs, public postmortems), producing a shareable teardown page per case: the bet as stated, the evidence at the time, the precedent cases, what the track record would have flagged, honest confidence score. (b) **The hook:** "Get your own bet torn down." Submitting a real product decision (form, 3 fields) jumps your waitlist position and queues a private teardown for the best submissions. Sharing spam doesn't move your position; skin in the game does.

**Why it can pop.** PM Twitter/LinkedIn runs on hindsight takes about famous product decisions; we bring an artifact instead of an opinion, and the artifact demonstrates the product. The submit-your-bet mechanic converts lurkers into discovery data and design-partner pipeline: everyone who submits a real bet has self-identified as feeling the pain.

**Execution.**

1. Day 0-1: pick 3 cases with rich PUBLIC paper trails (candidates: a well-documented pricing change, a public API deprecation walk-back, a publicly postmortemed rewrite). Rule: autopsy the DECISION, cite only public sources, never individuals, no recent layoffs or tragedies, tone = respectful engineering analysis, each ends with "what a track record would have caught" and "what they got right."
2. Day 1-2: run the Critic on each for real (the teardowns must be genuine product output, [PROVEN]); founder edits for taste and fairness; build the teardown share pages; wire the submit-a-bet form + waitlist position logic.
3. Day 4: Autopsy #1 fires as an X thread + LinkedIn post + the share page. Day 5: #2. Day 6 or the listing week: #3, based on reception.
4. Ongoing: every submitted bet gets tagged in the discovery tracker (03); the 25 best submitters get personal outreach as design-partner candidates.

**Assets needed:** 3 finished teardowns, share-page template, submission form, position-jump logic, thread copy per case.

**Success metric:** one autopsy clears 50k impressions or 200 waitlist referrals; ≥100 real bets submitted by day 7; ≥10 submitters convert to design-partner calls.

**Kill criteria:** if Autopsy #1 lands flat AND draws fairness criticism, rewrite #2 as a self-autopsy of one of our own recorded wrong decisions (the track record has real ones) and test the honesty angle instead.

### Brief 3 — "It built part of itself" evidence drop (the opening shot, day 3)

**The idea.** A 60-90 second screen recording plus an X thread and LinkedIn post: the actual trace of Supaprod taking a spec through its own gated path to a merged PR. Not a claim, evidence: the mission trace, the approval gate, the CI check, the merge, on screen, uncut. Caption frame: "Engineers got agents. Product teams got chatbots that draft and wait. So we built the other thing. Here it is shipping part of itself." [PROVEN]

**Why it can pop.** "The product built itself" is the single most screenshot-able true sentence we own, the engineer-envy frame travels in both the builder and PM communities, and it sets the tone for everything after: this account posts evidence, not demos.

**Execution.**

1. Day 0-1: pick the best real merged PR from the gated path; re-record the trace walkthrough cleanly (real data, no staging); cut to under 90 seconds; end frame = Launch Track record URL + waitlist.
2. Day 2: founder writes the thread in his own voice (8-10 posts: the problem, the evidence video, what's real today with live numbers, the honest limitations, the waitlist ask). No superlatives; the numbers do the talking.
3. Day 3, morning US time: fires on X and LinkedIn simultaneously; seeded into 2-3 communities where self-posting is allowed (per channel playbook 01, respecting each community's rules); pinned to profile all week.

**Assets needed:** the recording, thread copy, LinkedIn version, the failure-path GIF as the first reply (the pre-empt: "and here is what happens when it gets one wrong").

**Success metric:** 250+ waitlist signups attributed to day 3-4; the thread outperforms every prior account post by 10x; at least 5 DMs from product people asking for access.

**Kill criteria:** none this week (it is the opening shot either way); if it underperforms, the learning goes into re-cutting the video for the HN week, where it becomes the demo asset.

### Brief 4 — Founder week arc: "I let agents run my product org for a week" (days 3-7)

**The idea.** Five daily posts, each one the REAL morning brief from the founder's workspace: what the agents did overnight (missions advanced, signals clustered, drafts produced), what they got wrong, what got reverted, what the founder overrode and why. Ends day 7 with the week-in-evidence recap and the running totals. The arc format gives people a reason to follow rather than scroll past once, and it demonstrates the while-you-sleep layer no screenshot can.

**Why it can pop.** Build-in-public is saturated with "what I shipped"; nobody posts "what my agents decided while I slept, including the mistake." The honesty compounds: by day 4, replies start asking "can it do this for MY backlog," which is the design-partner funnel doing its own work.

**Execution.**

1. Each morning days 3-7: screenshot the real brief, pick ONE interesting decision + ONE imperfection, write 4-6 sentences in the founder's voice, link the Launch Track record.
2. Day 5: the mid-arc twist post, the best wrong call of the week and its revert evidence, paired with the failure-path GIF.
3. Day 7: recap with the week's totals (missions, decisions, reverts, waitlist count if strong) + "25 design-partner seats, first sessions are with me" ask.

**Assets needed:** none beyond daily screenshots and 20 minutes of founder voice per day. Lowest-effort item on the board.

**Success metric:** follower growth 3x baseline over the arc; ≥10 inbound "can I try this" DMs; day-7 recap is the week's top post.

**Kill criteria:** if days 3-4 get no traction, tighten to 2 more posts (day 5 twist + day 7 recap) instead of daily, and push the effort into Briefs 2 and 5.

### Brief 5 — Micro-influencer personal teardowns (the seeded fuse)

**The idea.** Hand-pick 10 PM/product voices (mid-size: 5k-80k followers, high engagement, writes about product craft or AI tooling; sourced via the prospecting playbook in 03 + HyperAgent research rig). Each gets a personal note from the founder + early access + a genuine Critic teardown of one publicly visible bet from THEIR product or content, delivered privately first, no strings. If it's good, they share it because it's about them and it's substantive. That is the whole mechanic: make the first 10 shares personal, earned, and impossible for an audience to distinguish from organic (because they ARE organic).

**Why it can pop.** Ten genuine reactions from trusted PM voices beats any amount of self-posting, and each teardown doubles as a product demo in the recipient's own context. Worst case: 10 high-quality design-partner conversations with people who influence thousands of our exact ICP.

**Execution.**

1. Day 0-1: build the list of 10 (criteria above; no mega-accounts, they don't reply); founder approves.
2. Day 1-2: run and edit 10 teardowns (batch through the product, founder QA for taste; ~30 min each after the first).
3. Day 2-3: founder sends 10 personal DMs/emails: "I built an agent that red-teams product bets. I ran it on [their public bet]. Here's what it found, thought you'd want to see it either way. If it's wrong, tell me where." No ask to share. Optional PS: early access link.
4. Days 4-7: respond to every reply personally; offer design-partner seats to the engaged; NEVER ask them to post.

**Assets needed:** the list, 10 teardowns, DM template (personalized per recipient), early-access links.

**Success metric:** ≥6/10 reply; ≥3 share something publicly; ≥4 take a design-partner or feedback call.

**Kill criteria:** if the first 3 sends get no reply in 48h, rewrite the note (shorter, lead with their bet not our product) before sending the remaining 7.

## 5. Supporting fires (quick specs)

- **Failure-path GIF (asset, day 0-1):** 15-20 seconds, wrong AI-drafted call, the revert keystroke visible, the track record evidence landing. Used as first-reply on every major post. Skeptics judge the error path; give it to them first. (Same brief as [launch-assets](../pitch/launch-assets.md) §1; one recording serves both weeks.)
- **Skin-in-the-game waitlist (mechanic, day 1-2):** three tiers: join (email), jump (submit a real product bet), front of line (book a design-partner call). Position moves for substance, not spam. Every jump = discovery data.
- **Worst-decision challenge (day 5 open, resolve in listing week):** "Submit the worst product decision you ever witnessed (anonymized). The Critic autopsies the top 10 publicly." Bridges the wave into the HN week with UGC.
- **"The ICE table that never learns" (day 6 or listing week):** a single scrolling page: a static prioritization table that never changes vs a track record where recorded outcomes visibly re-rank the next bets [PROVEN, RF seam]. Category contrast without naming competitors. The empty-cell line: "Every prioritization tool scores your bets once. None of them checks whether the score was right. That check is the product."
- **Meme pack (drips days 3-7):** 6-8 formats on the agent-envy gap ("2026 engineers: my agents shipped overnight. 2026 PMs: my chatbot drafted a doc and is waiting"). Founder-voice, self-aware, never punching at people. One per day max; memes season the wave, they are not the wave.
- **AI-announcement hijack kit (armed, fires on trigger):** pre-approved response templates for the next big model/agent launch: "The labs keep shipping the hands. The seat above the fleet, the one that decides, approves, and learns from outcomes, is still empty. That's the one we built." Fires within 2 hours of a major announcement or not at all.
- **Evidence Page (day 2, part of the homepage):** live counters from the founder workspace: AI events, missions, decisions, learnings, reverts. Caption: "We ran it on ourselves for ten weeks before showing you."
- **ARD open spec (start now, land in listing week):** "ADRs gave engineers decision records. Product decisions still live in Slack archaeology." Publish the Architecture-of-Decision-Records spec for product (schema + examples) on GitHub; Supaprod is the reference implementation. Compounds into the HN conversation.
- **X Spaces / LinkedIn Live (day 6-7):** 30 minutes, "watch the track record live": walk the Launch Track record, take one audience-submitted bet, run the Critic on it live. Co-host = the friendliest voice from Brief 5 if one emerged.
- **PM-course cohort seeding (send day 2, slow burn):** offer Reforge/Product School/Exponent alumni communities a free live "decision autopsy workshop" (value-first; product appears as the tool used, not the pitch).

## 7. What we do NOT do

- No engagement-bait threads, no "RT to win access", no follower loops. They fill the waitlist with the wrong people and poison the pull signal we need for YC.
- No paid ads this week. The wave's job is to prove organic pull exists; ads would blind that instrument.
- No punching at named competitors or individuals. The category contrast (static scores vs track record) does the work without a target.
- No burning Show HN or Product Hunt. Those cards fire once, in the listing week, on top of the proof this wave generates.
- Nothing outward without founder approval. Standing rule, no exceptions.
