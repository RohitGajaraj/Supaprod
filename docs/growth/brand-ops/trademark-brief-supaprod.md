## 3. The mark

**SUPAPROD** — standard-character word mark. A stylized/logo mark may follow later; we would like counsel's advice on sequencing (word mark first is our assumption).

- **Construction:** coined term — "supa" (a phonetic respelling of "super") + "prod" (evoking both "**prod**uct", the customer domain, and "**prod**uction", the software-industry term for the live environment software ships to).
- **Pronunciation:** SOO-pah-prod.
- **Linguistic notes surfaced in research** (for counsel's completeness, none believed blocking):
  - "Supaprod" coincides with a **Thai surname** — a private Instagram account @supaprod belongs to an individual named Janjira Supaprod (https://www.instagram.com/supaprod/). "Supa-" is a common auspicious Thai name prefix.
  - In French, "la prod" is common slang for a music/film production; the term is widespread in music-producer culture generally (see §6(e)).
  - Capital-P "Prod" is documented sectarian slang for a Protestant in Northern Ireland/Scotland (https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Prod; PSNI language guidance as reported by irishnews.com). Context-bound and, in our lay view, near-invisible inside the compound; noted for completeness.
  - No negative meaning found in Spanish, German, or Hindi.
- **Distinctiveness (our lay view, to be confirmed):** suggestive rather than merely descriptive for the goods in §4 — "prod" does not immediately describe a function of product-management software; a mental step is required. We ask counsel to confirm the §2(e)(1) posture.

## 4. The business and the goods/services

**Plain-English description of the business:** an AI software platform (an "Agentic Product OS") for product managers, founders, and product teams. It captures product decisions together with their reasoning into a queryable memory graph; generates discovery insights, requirements documents, and roadmaps; and runs AI agents that autonomously build, review, and deploy software. It is B2B SaaS, delivered as a web application, targeting the US market first.

### (a) SUPERPROD — Superprod Group SAS (the key item)

- **Who they are:** French audiovisual/animation production group founded 2010 by former Gaumont executives; world-class animation studios in Paris, Angoulême, and Milan; **offices in Los Angeles and New York**; roughly 200–500 staff (one tracker reports 471 staff and ~$120M revenue); production partners include Netflix, HBO Max, and Warner Bros Animation; subsidiaries include **Superights** (distribution) and **440 Hz** (a music-rights platform — i.e., software-adjacent services); acquired Studio 352/Mélusine in 2024.
  - Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superprod_Group · https://superprod.net/en/the-group · https://growjo.com/company/SUPERPROD · trade-press coverage of the LA office (awn.com) and the 2024 acquisition (deadline.com).
- **Why it matters:** SUPAPROD and SUPERPROD are **aurally near-identical** — in most English speech "supa" is exactly how "super" sounds — and one letter apart visually. Examiners treat sound-alike marks as similar in likelihood-of-confusion analysis. Their US commercial presence (LA/NY offices) gives them standing to care.
- **Our register finding:** a site-restricted search of Justia's USPTO mirror returned **no SUPERPROD mark on the US federal register** (nearest hits: SUPERMEGA, SUPER PRENEUR), and we could not find any USPTO filing by the group. Their protectable footprint is presumably **French (INPI) and/or EU (EUIPO)** in entertainment classes — class 41 certainly, likely class 9 (recorded/downloadable media, which is standard for studios), possibly 35/38. **We could not verify this** (TMview/EUIPO blocked).
- **Requests to counsel:** (i) confirm the US register is clear of SUPERPROD; (ii) enumerate the group's INPI/EUIPO/WIPO portfolio with exact classes; (iii) opine on §2(d)/opposition exposure in the US, and on EU exposure for our later, deferred EU filing.
- **Our lay analysis, for counsel to test:** with no US registration, US examiner-refusal risk is approximately zero (examiners cite registered marks); a common-law/confusion action would have to argue that buyers of B2B product-management SaaS are confused with buyers of children's-animation production services — different services, channels, and purchasers; a weak case despite the aural identity. EU exposure concentrates in class 9 — hence our EU strategy (§7) of filing **class 42 only**, if and when triggered.

### (b) SUPABASE — Supabase Inc.

- **Who they are:** major open-source database platform company (Y Combinator S20); raised at a **~$10.5B valuation in June 2026** (https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/04/database-startup-supabase-raises-500-million-10point5-billion-valuation.html).
- **Why it matters:** our mark uses the "supa-" prefix that Supabase's own name popularized, and — full disclosure for counsel's context — our product is technically **built on Supabase infrastructure**. We intend no affiliation claim, and our internal copy rules prohibit any "Supabase for X" framing.
- **Our findings:** Supabase holds SUPABASE registrations/filings; its published brand policy (https://supabase.com/brand-assets) protects the SUPABASE mark and logos only and asserts **nothing over the "supa-" prefix**. A large ecosystem of independent supa-* named tools exists with no enforcement history we could find; the closest precedent is **Supaglue**, a Y Combinator W23 company using the same supa+word formula, which operated unchallenged and was acquired by Stripe in March 2024 (TechCrunch, 2024-03-29).
- **Request to counsel:** confirm no SUPA-formative marks in classes 9/42 would block or materially narrow SUPAPROD.

### (c) SUPAPRO

- **What it is:** a live South African lead-management/CRM SaaS at https://www.supapro.com/ with an iOS App Store listing — one letter from SUPAPROD, in the broad software space.
- **Request to counsel:** assess its citation/confusion weight (we found no US registration for it).

### (d) SUPER — King.com Ltd, US Reg. 5135888, and the crowded SUPER-/SUPA-formative field

- King.com holds a registered SUPER mark in the software space (US Reg. 5135888), and the SUPER-/SUPA-formative field in classes 9/42 is generally crowded. Our lay understanding is that a crowded field cuts in favor of coexistence — each mark is weaker and consumers distinguish on smaller differences — which is how the many supa-/super- software brands already coexist. Counsel to confirm this reading and its effect on both registrability and the enforceable scope of SUPAPROD.
