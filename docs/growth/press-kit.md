# Press kit

> _Created: 2026-08-07 · Last updated: 2026-08-07_

**Everything a journalist, podcast producer, newsletter writer or directory editor needs, in one file.** Paste from here rather than composing, so two write-ups never contradict each other.

Two standing rules govern this file, and both come from canon.

1. **Nothing outward sends without the founder's approval.** Not a quote, not an asset, not a reply to a query. See [`README.md`](./README.md).
2. **Supaprod is pre-launch and says so.** Read §1 before using anything below. There is no traction section in this kit because there is no traction, and inventing one is the single fastest way to lose a story that is otherwise true.

Positioning source: the root [`../../README.md`](../../README.md). Ratified profile copy: [`brand-ops/social-accounts.md`](./brand-ops/social-accounts.md) §3. Assets: [`branding/README.md`](./branding/README.md).

## 1. The honest pre-launch position

**State this plainly whenever a piece needs a status line. It is not a caveat, it is the fact.**

> Supaprod has not launched. Public availability is planned for **mid-September 2026**. It has **no external users, no waitlist signups, no customers, no revenue and no funding to announce.** Billing is built, tested and deliberately switched off. Every account in production belongs to the founder or to internal testing. The company is not incorporated yet.

**Why that is a decision rather than an omission**, and the sentence to use if the piece wants one:

> The whole claim is that the loop closes, that a shipped outcome changes what you are shown next. You cannot validate that with half a loop, because you get feedback on a demo instead of on the thesis. So the engine was finished before the doors opened.

**What may never appear in a piece we supplied material for:**

| Never | Because |
| --- | --- |
| A customer, logo, testimonial or named user | None exists. |
| A revenue, ARR, growth or user-count figure | All of them are zero or internal, and a zero framed as momentum is still a fabrication. |
| A waitlist number | Zero waitlist signups have ever been recorded. |
| An award, ranking or accelerator badge | None held. |
| Page views, visits or impressions as evidence of demand | Traffic is not traction, and offering it as traction is the same fabrication in a softer wrapper. |
| "Launched", "in beta with", "used by" | Not true today. The verb is "pre-launch". |

If an outlet requires a traction section to run the piece, the correct answer is that we do not have one yet, and we would rather appear later with real numbers than early with borrowed ones.

## 2. Boilerplate, three lengths

All three carry the same claim and can be used interchangeably. **Do not mix them into a fourth version.** If a length is needed that is not here, ask rather than improvise.

### 25 words

> For product managers who ship with agents. Supaprod tells you what to build, builds and ships it, learns what worked, then guides your next call.

### 50 words

> Supaprod is where product decisions live when agents do the work. Agents run the product lifecycle end to end, from the first signal to shipped code, inside boundaries a human sets in advance. Every settled outcome is written back against the decision that caused it, so the next call arrives carrying evidence.

### 100 words

> Supaprod is where product decisions live when agents do the work, and it works in three layers. The director reads your signals, your product data, your competitors and your own past calls, and ranks what is worth doing next. The operating system runs the whole lifecycle across seven stations that agents walk on their own, inside boundaries a human sets in advance. The brain learns from what actually happened and then guides: a settled outcome is written back against the decision that caused it, and that re-ranks what you are shown next. Supaprod is pre-launch today, with public availability planned for mid-September 2026.

**The one line to lead with if a piece has room for only one:**

> Engineers got agents. Product people got chatbots. Supaprod is the operating system that closes that gap.

**Vocabulary that must survive editing.** The brain **learns, then guides**. It does not remember, store, log or archive, and there is no "searchable history" of it. Those words describe a filing cabinet, which any vendor can build; what compounds is joining a team's own decisions to its own outcomes, labelled over time. If an editor rewrites the claim into storage language, ask for the correction: it makes the product sound smaller than it is.

### The three layers, if a piece explains the architecture

Told in this order, always, and never all three in one headline: **door, then body, then brain.**

| | Layer | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **01** | The director | Tells you what to build. Reads your signals, product data, competitors and your own past calls, and ranks what is worth doing next. |
| **02** | The operating system | Runs the whole lifecycle. Seven stations that agents walk on their own, inside boundaries a human sets in advance. |
| **03** | The brain | Learns, and then guides. A settled outcome re-ranks what you are shown next. |

**Two things worth getting right in a technical piece.** The seven stations are a **route, not a conveyor**: work visits only the stations it needs and can enter at any of them, and a skipped station is recorded with its reason. And the build engine is **ours**, running frontier models through one governed chokepoint in the customer's own repository. We do not subcontract work to other coding tools, and a piece saying we do is wrong.

## 5. Approved quotes

**Five quotes, attributable to Rohit Gajaraj, founder and CEO of Supaprod.** Every one is about the mechanism, because that is what is true today. None claims a user, a result or a number. Use verbatim or ask; a paraphrase of a quote is not a quote.

> **On why the loop is the product.**
> "A loop that ends in a report is a dashboard. Ours ends by changing what you are shown next. That is the whole difference, and it is the reason this is an operating system rather than another view of your backlog."

> **On what actually compounds.**
> "Any vendor can hold your decisions. What compounds is joining them to your outcomes, labelled over time, so the next call arrives with evidence. No model has that, because it is not something you can buy or scrape. It is produced as a byproduct of running the loop."

> **On why this half of the work did not commoditise.**
> "Code has a fast oracle. It compiles in seconds, so an agent can iterate against it, and building got cheap. What to build, and whether it was right, gets feedback in weeks to quarters. The expensive half of product work is the half nobody automated, and it is the half that does not commoditise."

> **On governance, and why it is not an approval queue.**
> "Policy is set in advance and does not block. Permission is asked in the moment and does. If every step passes to a human for sign-off, you do not have agents, you have a queue with a nicer interface."

> **On how we decide something actually works.**
> "A green test proves the code does what the test says, and nothing more. We once found nine separately shipped features doing nothing in production, all of them passing the full suite. None was found by reading code. They were found by querying the live database, and now a detector catches the tenth."

**Quotes we will not give**, so nobody asks twice: anything about how many people use it, anything comparing us to a named competitor's product quality, and anything that describes the brain as a place where things are kept.

## 6. What Supaprod is not

**Half the clarity is here.** This category is crowded with things Supaprod resembles from a distance, and every one of these miscategorisations has been made by a smart reader already.

| Not | Why the distinction matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Not an issue tracker or a workspace.** | We do not try to be a better tracker. Trackers dispatch work. They do not verify whether the bet paid off, and that verification is the entire company. |
| **Not a product management suite.** | Roadmap and prioritisation surfaces are built for a human to fill in. Their information model assumes a person does the judgement; ours assumes an agent does the work under policy. |
| **Not a code generation tool, and not a competitor to one.** | Codegen races to zero against a fast oracle. Supaprod decides what deserves to be built and owns the outcome. Our build engine is our own, running frontier models by API in the customer's repository. |
| **Not a chatbot or a copilot.** | A copilot drafts and waits. Agents here run the lifecycle end to end inside boundaries set in advance, and stop only at the few things that cross them. |
| **Not a spec or PRD writer.** | Drafting is the cheap half and it commoditised first. It is one station out of seven. |
| **Not a design tool.** | Not a canvas, not a Figma competitor. Design is a station in the loop, checked against a live design system. |
| **Not an analytics or experimentation product.** | Those hold the number and never the decision that caused it. We read the result. We do not own the measurement. |
| **Not a filing system, archive or searchable history.** | The brain learns and then guides. Storage is not the claim, because storage is not defensible and it describes less than the product does. |
| **Not an "AI PM tool".** | The phrase puts a feature label on a substrate. We do not use it and we ask not to be described with it. |
| **Not launched.** | See §1. |

## 8. Usage rules for the mark and the wordmark

### What the mark is

A **seven-petal spiral**, an epitrochoid, revolving around a glowing core. The seven petals are the seven stations of the loop, drawn as one continuous curve so the lifecycle reads as a single connected journey rather than seven separate marks. The core is the intelligence the loop revolves around: an ember disc with a small gold bead at its heart.

The wordmark is **Supaprod** set in Geist Sans 600 with tight tracking. On hero moments across public surfaces it may be set in Geist Pixel Square, **one Pixel word per asset**, because Pixel everywhere stops being a signal and becomes a texture.

### Do

- Use the on-dark mark on dark grounds and the on-light mark on light grounds.
- Use the mono variant on photographs, in print, and in any one-colour context.
- Keep the core ember and gold, the one warm accent, even on mono spirals where colour is allowed.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least the diameter of the core on all sides. The vectors already build in roughly 10% padding.
- Respect minimum sizes: the mark never below **20px**, the lockup wordmark never below roughly **14px**.
- Regenerate rather than redraw, so the curve stays exact.

### Do not

- **Do not recolour the spiral.** It is near-white `#f2f0ed` lines with an ember core, everywhere. Blue and violet spirals are pre-2026-08-05 artifacts and are wrong; the source has a test that fails the build if that colour returns.
- **Do not rotate, stretch, skew or distort** the static mark.
- **Do not add effects.** No drop shadows beyond the built-in glow, no outlines, no gradients across the spiral.
- **Do not place the full-colour mark on a busy photograph.** Use the mono variant.
- **Do not redraw the petals by hand**, and do not trace the mark from a raster.
- **Do not set the wordmark in gold.** It is white on dark and near-black on light. Gold is the core bead and a caution accent, and no live surface sets type in it.
- **Do not letterspace, condense, outline or re-set the wordmark** in a substitute typeface. If Geist is unavailable, use a supplied lockup file.
- **Do not write `SupaProd`.** It is `Supaprod` in prose and `supaprod` in handles, domains and slugs.
- **Do not lock the mark up with another company's logo** to imply a partnership, integration or endorsement that does not exist.
- **Do not modify the mark to make a derivative logo**, a fan variant, or an event badge.

### Colour, if a piece needs the palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ember, the brand accent | `#FF6B2C` | The one brand colour. Primary action, and "needs a human". |
| Gold, the core bead | `#E8B44C` | The mark's living centre, and the caution accent. |
| Neutral, dark ground | `#0A0A0A` | The default background. |
| Neutral, light ground | `#FFFFFF` | Light background. |
| Text on dark | `#EDEDED` | |
| Text on light | `#111111` | |

Grayscale carries at least 90% of any surface. Colour appears only where it carries meaning.

**Blue `#3E63DD` exists in the product interface and must never appear in a brand asset.** A blue or violet mark means you are holding a retired file.

The name and mark are used as identifiers of Supaprod and its product. Editorial use in reporting about the company is fine and needs no permission. Any other use, including in a product, on merchandise, in an advertisement, or in a way that suggests endorsement, needs written permission from **hello@supaprod.ai**.
