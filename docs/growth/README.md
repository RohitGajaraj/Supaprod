## The one-paragraph summary

The engine is finished and market contact is zero, so the week's only job is putting Supaprod in front of real product people and making them love it. Days 0–2 build the launch surface (waitlist with the teardown hook, homepage, assets, prospect list). Days 3–7 fire the wave: the honest founder story, the "it built part of itself" receipts, public Critic teardowns, and give-first DMs — converting attention into booked design-partner sessions, not just signups. The Show HN and Product Hunt cards stay reserved for week 2–3, after the no-signup demo and 2–3 real beta stories exist. Success is measured on tiered targets (Floor / Strong / Breakout in [`00`](./00-launch-operating-manual.md) §0), with paying intent and week-2 return outranking any waitlist count.
