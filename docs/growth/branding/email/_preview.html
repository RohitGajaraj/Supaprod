Answers to what you asked

Lighter ember: done, and this is the ceiling. White measured on each candidate: #C24E1E 4.77, #C6501E 4.60 (shipped), #CB5420 4.35, #D2551F 4.15. The binding constraint is the 11px mono kicker, which is small text and needs 4.5:1. The 23px bold headline is large text and would survive to 3:1, so going lighter means growing the kicker until it qualifies. That is a real option, not a quiet one.

Pattern in Gmail: yes, and safely. A tiled background on the band cell, with bgcolor underneath as the fallback. Clients that ignore it (Outlook without VML) or block it until the reader clicks see the flat band we already had, so the worst case is the status quo. Kept at 5 to 9 percent white: a texture a reader notices behind body copy has failed.

The logo error was real and is not a design problem. .gitignore carries a blanket *.png, so the new marks were never committed and 404 on the origin. Force-added now. It will render after the next deploy, and the check is curl -I https://supaprod.ai/mark-white.png. Four other live-referenced assets were found 404 the same way: icon-192, icon-512, favicon-16, favicon-32, which also broke the PWA manifest.

Mobile: yes, considered from the start. No media queries, because mail clients strip <style> blocks and a media query in a head that gets removed is decoration. Instead the shell is fluid: max-width rather than width, percentage table width, no fixed columns, and a headline that reflows. The band survives a 320px screen. What it does NOT survive is Outlook desktop ignoring border-radius, which degrades to square corners, and that is acceptable.
