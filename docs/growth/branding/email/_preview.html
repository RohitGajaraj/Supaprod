Why there is no starfield in either

CSS background images are among the worst supported features in email. Outlook needs VML to draw one at all, and most clients block remote images until the reader clicks. A starfield would therefore be absent for the majority of first opens, leaving whatever colour sits underneath it. The landing page can carry the orrery because a browser is not a mail client.

Dark email has a second trap: Gmail on Android force-inverts colours, and Outlook Windows ignores dark styling, so B will not look like B everywhere. It is achievable and can read premium, but it must be built to survive being flattened, not previewed once and shipped.
