## The finding (F-89)

**An e2e spec wrote 10 real tracks into production** (not 3 as first counted). **8 of the 10
sweep drives since 13:20 went to press tracks** — the Round-8 pattern repeated exactly: a spec
pressing the production database creates duplicate tracks that consume the sweep. The track

## The reversal (F-90)

**`is_sample` was documented backwards everywhere.** The code (track-tick.ts:85) builds its
exclusion from `sampleWorkspaceIds` selecting `.eq("is_sample", true)` and passes to
`.not("workspace_id", "in", excluded)` — **so `is_sample = true` means a demo fixture that the
sweep SKIPS**, not "the sweep may drive here" as CLAUDE.md and the operating model claimed.

**The reversal is also the fix for F-89:** an `is_sample = true` workspace is exactly where an
e2e spec should write, because the sweep **provably will not drive it**. Workspace
`b90da531-34aa-4009-bcce-2162b87f50ac` ("Sample sandbox") is now named in the operating model
as the guarded workspace, with the measurement showing why: a phase-3 run wrote 10 real tracks
into the live workspace, and 8 of the next 10 sweep drives went to them.
