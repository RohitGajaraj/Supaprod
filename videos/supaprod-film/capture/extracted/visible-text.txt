[span] How it works
[h2] One system, three layers.
[span] the director
[h3] It tells you what to build.
[p] Reads your feedback, your data, your competitors, the market. Names what is worth building next, evidence attached. Your product taste becomes a system.
[span] the loop
[h3] It runs the whole lifecycle.
[p] Two ways in: start at Discover when the problem is new, or Build and Learn when it is cheaper to try than to argue. Either way the loop closes, and what you learn re-ranks what comes next.
[span] the shared brain
[h3] It learns, and it guides.
[p] Every decision is recorded with its evidence, then graded against what actually happened. It compounds. Next time it tells you what is right, and warns you before you repeat what was wrong.
