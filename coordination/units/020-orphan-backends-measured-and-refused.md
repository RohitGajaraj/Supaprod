Unit 020: the orphan-backend queue measured and died; two salvage atoms survived it

This unit is mostly refusals, which is the point. Units 019's closing note
queued four zero-consumer backends for wiring. Two read-only scouts then
measured all four against what ships, what tests ban, and what doctrine
says, and the queue did not survive contact. Full evidence with file:line
went to MAIN LANE in requests/008; the short form lives here so the next
reader does not re-derive it.

getTodayLanes: refused wholesale. Wrong-workspace scoping by construction,
a push-filter regression a build-failing test exists to prevent, a
cross-workspace leak in its calibration read, double the query cost, and an
audit that already called it worse than what shipped. Salvage: the watch /
risk lane idea (net-new capability if re-derived with correct scoping and
deduped against existing gate items) and actor attribution for the crew we
already render.

getLoopPulse: refused, deletion recommended. Tried on the old Today hero,
removed by the recompose that defined today's scope, superseded twice over,
and shaped exactly like the number-without-taxonomy tile this route's own
doc bans.

getRecentExecutedUnattended: parked, real. Unique data, wrong moment;
deserves its own unit beside the governance narrative with the banned word
reinvented out of the heading.

getBriefing: parked, real, homeless. Built for the Thread column, which does
not exist; every current /today slot fails doctrine on contact; carries
banned vocabulary in exported names that is free to rename while importers
stay at zero; collides in naming with the snooze-fed tomorrow's brief.
